PRODUCT MARKETING BRIEF
Product: Aurora Trail 2 — trail running shoe
Launch date: 2026-11-15 (comms/teaser starts 2026-10-01)
Markets: US (en), Germany (de), Japan (ja)
All three markets launch the same day. Sizing is shown in US, EU, and JP
(cm) depending on market; the spec sheet is otherwise identical everywhere.

Context
Aurora Trail 2 is the second generation of the Aurora Trail, and the third
shoe in the Aurora line after Aurora Road 5 and Aurora Trail 1. Aurora
Trail 1 stays on sale while stock lasts. This page should link out to both
under a "More from the Aurora line" section near the bottom.

Target audience
Weekend trail runners, 25-40, who've outgrown road-running shoes but
aren't ultra racers. They're gear-curious, price-sensitive above $160, and
influenced by reviews more than ads.
Users can join the waitlist from the hero or at the end of the page.

Core benefits

- Dual-density foam midsole — cushioned on descents, stable on climbs
- Recycled-mesh upper (60% post-consumer content) — breathable, durable
- 4mm lugs, sticky rubber outsole — reliable grip on wet rock and roots

Value proposition
What we're selling: a trail shoe that doesn't force a tradeoff between
cushioning and stability.
Why it matters: most shoes at this price pick one or the other.
The payoff: fewer rolled ankles on technical descents, less fatigue on
long days.

What's in the box

- The shoes (colorway options below)
- Removable 5mm insole, fitted, swaps out for an orthotic
- Spare set of laces in a contrast color
- Recycled-content shoe bag
- Sizing and care card

Colorways
Slate/ember, moss/bone, and black/black at launch. Merchandising maintains
the colorway list centrally since it's shared across every shoe in the
Aurora line and rotates seasonally (two more colorways are already booked
for spring).

Price
$150 in slate/ember and black/black, $160 in moss/bone (the undyed mesh in
that colorway is sourced in smaller batches). Free shipping over $100,
which this qualifies for either way. Aurora Trail 1 stays listed at $115
while stock lasts.

Customer reviews
Three customers agreed to be quoted and named: - Marisol Vega, a physical therapist in Boulder, moved off road shoes
after a season of shin splints. Five stars, says the descents are
where the difference shows up. - Dev Okonkwo, a cross-country coach in Asheville, bought a pair after
wet-root season sidelined two runners on the team. Five stars, has
since bought a second pair. - Hana Lindqvist, a weekend runner in Portland, runs wide. Four stars,
wishes the toe box ran a half size wider and wants a waterproof
version.

Specs

Drop: 6mm
Stack height: 32mm heel, 26mm forefoot
Outsole: 4mm lugs, sticky rubber compound
Upper: recycled mesh, 60% post-consumer content
Sizing: US 6-14 in half sizes, EU and JP (cm) equivalents per market
Fit: standard width only at launch
Warranty: 1 year materials, 6 months against outsole delamination
